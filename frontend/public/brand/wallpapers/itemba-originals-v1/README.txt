ITEMBA Originals - Volume 01

Five original wallpapers for ITEMBA OS: Dune Light, Tidal Glass, Aurora Veil, Pearl Fold, Midnight Rift.

Choose a background in Control centre > Open Appearance Studio > ITEMBA Originals. Your selection syncs to your account. Colour themes are selected separately.

Original artwork was generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 25 September 2026. Each original PNG is 1672 x 941 pixels. Desktop WebP files retain those dimensions; images were not enlarged or labelled as 4K. Small thumbnails are provided separately for the picker.

originals/: full-quality generated PNG artwork
*.webp: optimised desktop images
thumbnails/: picker previews
manifest.json: stable IDs, dimensions, display file sizes and SHA-256 checksums
PROMPTS.md: final generation prompts and integration notes
