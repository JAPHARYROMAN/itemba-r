# Role-Based Training Paths

This document defines the required training for each major role in ITEMBA-R. Each path specifies the courses to complete, manuals to read, walkthroughs to try, and the estimated total training time.

---

## 1. Group Director (~2 hours)

**Profile:** Senior leadership; needs strategic overview, not operational detail.

### Required Courses
- **Course 1:** ITEMBA-R Overview for Executives (30 min)
  - Group structure, company overview, dashboard navigation
- **Course 2:** Group Control & Sensitive Records (20 min)
  - Bank accounts, loans, fixed assets, contracts, documents vault
- **Course 3:** Approvals & Sign-Off Workflows (20 min)
  - Approving POs, payroll, period close, launch sign-off

### Manuals to Read
- `getting-started.md` — Sections 1–7
- `group-control-user-guide.md` — Sections 1–3, 8–9
- `approvals-controls-user-guide.md` — Sections 3–4

### Walkthroughs to Complete
- Walkthrough: "Review and Approve a High-Value Purchase Order"
- Walkthrough: "Navigate the Executive Dashboard"

### Competency Checkpoint
- Can navigate to Group Control → Bank Accounts
- Can approve a pending approval from the Approvals dashboard
- Can run the Group P&L report

---

## 2. Finance Controller (~3 hours)

**Profile:** Responsible for financial integrity across all companies.

### Required Courses
- **Course 1:** Finance & Accounting Engine (60 min)
  - COA setup, journal entries, posting rules, period management
- **Course 2:** Accounts Receivable & Payable (30 min)
  - Invoices, payments, aging reports
- **Course 3:** Payroll Review & Approval (30 min)
  - Payroll generation, review, approval, GL reconciliation
- **Course 4:** Tax & Compliance for Finance (20 min)
  - VAT accounting, PAYE, WHT recording, filing periods

### Manuals to Read
- `finance-accounting-user-guide.md` — All sections
- `hr-payroll-user-guide.md` — Sections 7–10
- `compliance-tax-user-guide.md` — Sections 1–5

### Walkthroughs to Complete
- Walkthrough: "Post a Journal Entry and Verify in Trial Balance"
- Walkthrough: "Complete a Bank Reconciliation"
- Walkthrough: "Close a Fiscal Period"

### Live Session
- 30-minute live session with IT Admin covering period close procedure and GL reconciliation.

### Competency Checkpoint
- Post a balanced journal entry
- Run the Trial Balance and verify it balances
- Complete a bank reconciliation for a test account

**Profile:** Fuel station operations — daily shifts, deliveries, collections.

### Required Courses
- **Course 1:** Petroleum Operations — Shifts and Collections (60 min)
  - Opening a shift, recording nozzle readings, recording collections, closing a shift
- **Course 2:** Tank Dips and Fuel Deliveries (20 min)
- **Course 3:** Credit Sales and Corporate Accounts (15 min)
- **Course 4:** Truck Parking Facility (15 min)
  - Recording vehicle entry/exit, collecting parking fees

### Manuals to Read
- `petroleum-user-guide.md` — All sections
- `rentals-parking-user-guide.md` — Part B (Truck Parking)

### Walkthroughs to Complete
- Walkthrough: "Open and Close a Fuel Shift"
- Walkthrough: "Record a Fuel Delivery"

### Competency Checkpoint
- Can open a fuel shift for 4 nozzles
- Can record closing readings and collections
- Can close the shift and explain the variance
