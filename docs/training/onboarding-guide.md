# Onboarding Guide — New User Onboarding for ITEMBA-R

## Overview

Welcome to ITEMBA-R! This guide walks you through everything you need to do to get started — from your first login to completing your training and understanding your first week tasks.

---

## Step 1: Account Setup by Admin

Before you can access ITEMBA-R, your IT Administrator must create your account and assign your role. If you have not received your login credentials, contact your IT Administrator directly.

**What the IT Admin sets up for you:**
- Your email address (username)
- A temporary password (you will change this on first login)
- Your role(s) — which modules and actions you can access
- Your company assignment

---

## Step 2: First Login

1. Open your browser and navigate to the ITEMBA-R URL (provided by your IT Admin).
2. Enter your email address and the temporary password.
3. Click **Sign In**.
4. You will be immediately prompted to **change your password**:
   - Choose a password at least 10 characters long
   - Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and a special character
   - Do not use your name, email, or obvious words
5. Click **Save New Password**.
6. You are now logged in.

> **Write down your new password or store it in a secure password manager.** If you forget your password, contact your IT Admin — there is no self-service reset currently.

---

## Step 3: Completing Your Profile

After first login, complete your profile before doing anything else:

1. Click your name or avatar in the top-right corner.
2. Select **My Profile**.
3. Verify your display name is correct.
4. Add your phone number (used for SMS notifications if configured).
5. Add your job title.
6. Upload a profile photo (optional but recommended — helps colleagues recognize you).
7. Under the **Notifications** tab, configure which notifications you want to receive.
8. Click **Save Changes**.

---

## Step 4: Dashboard Orientation

Your dashboard is your home base in ITEMBA-R. Take 5 minutes to explore it:

1. The **top row of StatCards** shows your key metrics. Hover over each to see what they measure.
2. The **sidebar** on the left is how you navigate between modules. Find the sections relevant to your role.
3. The **Notifications bell** in the top bar shows any pending items that need your attention.
4. The **Approvals** menu item shows items waiting for your approval (if applicable to your role).

> **If you see a module that appears greyed out**, it means you don't have access to it. This is normal — your role determines which sections you can use. If you believe you're missing access to something you need, contact your IT Admin.

---

## Step 5: First Training Course

ITEMBA-R includes built-in training courses designed for your role. **Complete your assigned training before going live with real transactions.**

1. Navigate to **Help & Training → Training Courses**.
2. You will see courses assigned to your role.
3. Click on the first course in your learning path.
4. Complete all the steps in the course.
5. At the end of each course, complete the practical tasks.
6. Navigate to **Help & Training → My Training** to track your overall progress.

Your training is tracked — your manager and the IT Admin can see which courses you have completed.

## Getting Help

### Help Center
Navigate to **Help & Training → Help Center** for:
- User manuals (written guides for each module)
- Help articles (answers to common questions)
- Walkthroughs (in-system guided tours)

Use the search bar to find answers quickly.

### Support Tickets
If you have a problem that isn't covered in the Help Center:
1. Navigate to **Support → My Tickets → New Ticket**.
2. Select the category that matches your issue.
3. Describe the problem clearly (include what you were trying to do and what happened).
4. Attach a screenshot if helpful.
5. Submit — you will receive a response within the SLA timeframe.

### Who to Contact

| Issue | Contact |
|---|---|
| Can't log in / account locked | IT Administrator |
| Missing access to a module | IT Administrator |
| Wrong information on a record | Your manager or Finance team |
| System error / crash | IT Administrator → Submit a support ticket |
| Training question | Your Training Coordinator |
| Payroll question | HR Manager |
| Compliance deadline question | Compliance Officer |

### Urgent Issues
For urgent issues (system down, can't open a fuel shift, can't process payments), contact the IT Administrator directly using the phone number provided during onboarding. Do not wait for a support ticket response for urgent operational issues.
